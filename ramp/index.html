RAMP Programme

RAMP is a Government of India initiative that develops high-potential MSMEs into "Champion MSMEs" through structured advisory covering growth planning, market access, credit and scheme support, export readiness, compliance, sustainability, and technology adoption.

Support provided

  • 1Structured business acceleration — customised growth roadmap, benchmarking, and measurable milestones
  • 2Market access & expansion — domestic/export linkages, buyer–seller connect, branding advisory
  • 3Credit & scheme enablement — help accessing central/state schemes, subsidies, and loans
  • 4Export & internationalization — export readiness, new-market entry, product diversification
  • 5Compliance & governance — Udyam, GST and financial compliance review, internal controls
  • 6Sustainability & certification — ESG guidance, efficiency advisory, ZED/ISO/FSSAI readiness
  • 7Innovation & technology — R&D and tech-partner access, IP awareness, digital tool adoption
  • 8Dedicated handholding — one-on-one mentoring, quarterly monitoring, leadership & digital training

Who this fits

  • High-potential service companies
  • Export-oriented units
  • High-growth manufacturing or service enterprises
  • Women-led businesses
  • MSMEs aiming for scale-up or diversification
  • Units seeking sustainability and certification alignment
